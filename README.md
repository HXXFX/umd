# UMD — Ultimate Machine Deathmatch

**Play it: <https://umd.windnoise.org/>**

Build a fighting robot out of real robot parts, and fight it in an octagon.

You are the corner, not the fighter. Buy parts, motors, fight software and an AI brain, put the machine together on the stage, and send
it into the cage against a challenger of its own weight. Before the bell you pick a plan; during the round you shout; between rounds you
tell your machine what to do differently. A fight runs until one machine is destroyed.

It runs in a current desktop browser (made and tested in Chrome). Your machines, your money and your record are kept in the browser you
play in, on that computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

The robot part shapes are derived from the unitree_ros robot descriptions (BSD-3-Clause): [LICENSE-unitree_ros.txt](LICENSE-unitree_ros.txt).
